Legal alternatives to Moviesda and Isaidub

Looking for a Tamil film or a Tamil-dubbed release? Start with the film’s title and release year, then check a licensed service for the audio track you want. AfterShow is not affiliated with Moviesda or Isaidub.

Where to look

JustWatch India
Search by title to compare streaming, rental, and purchase options in India. If you live elsewhere, switch to your country. Confirm the audio language on the streaming service itself.
Prime Video
Check the title page to see whether the film is included with a subscription or costs extra. Look for Tamil under audio languages before renting or buying.
Netflix India
If a title is in your catalog, open the player’s Audio & Subtitles menu. Tamil listed under subtitles does not mean the film is dubbed in Tamil.

If the film isn’t available

Try the original title or a different English spelling, and check that the release year matches. For a new release, look for a streaming date announced by the studio or distributor. Offers and audio tracks can change by country, service, and edition.

If you’re looking for a free option, check the rights holder’s official YouTube channel. Make sure it’s the full film rather than a trailer or a clip.
